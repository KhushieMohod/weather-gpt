IMD East Coast Marine Cyclone Advisory

Issued for coastal districts along the Bay of Bengal and the east coast of India. A developing low-pressure area may intensify over the next 24 to 48 hours. Residents and marine operators should follow the latest official bulletins from the India Meteorological Department and local disaster authorities.

Hazards: strengthening winds may reach dangerous velocities near the system center and along exposed coastal waters. Heavy rainfall can reduce visibility, flood low-lying roads, and create sudden drainage flows. Squally weather and rough to very rough seas may affect fishing grounds, ports, and ferry routes.

Marine safety: fishing vessels should not venture into the warned area. Vessels already at sea should return to the nearest safe harbor when instructed, secure loose equipment, maintain radio watch, and follow Coast Guard and port control directions. Operators must not rely on informal social media updates when an official warning is active.

Community safety routes: coastal residents should identify the nearest elevated shelter, keep evacuation routes clear, carry essential medicines and drinking water, and move early if local authorities issue an evacuation order. Avoid walking or driving through flooded underpasses, tidal channels, or roads blocked by fallen trees and power lines. Emergency teams should keep relief centers, communications equipment, and medical transport ready.

This advisory is an educational prototype document. Always verify current warnings, observed wind values, cyclone tracks, and evacuation orders with official IMD, Coast Guard, district administration, and state disaster management sources.
